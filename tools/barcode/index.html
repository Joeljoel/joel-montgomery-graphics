Barcode Data Merge

Creates Code 128B text for Libre Barcode 128. Existing columns and values are kept; a formatted barcode column is added. Apply the barcode font to that field in InDesign. Import the tab-delimited TXT as Unicode.

Prepare barcodes for InDesign

  1. Drop in a CSV, TSV or TXT file, or paste spreadsheet cells. Choose the separator and indicate whether the first row contains headings.
  2. Choose the source column that holds your codes and name the new barcode column. The export keeps the original columns.
  3. Select Download for InDesign. Import the tab-delimited TXT as Unicode in Data Merge and apply Libre Barcode 128 to the merged barcode field.

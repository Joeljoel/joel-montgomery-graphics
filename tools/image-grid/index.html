Get images from Easil
  1. Drag Copy image links to your browser’s bookmarks bar (one-time setup).
  2. Open your image list in Easil, scroll to load the images you want, then click that bookmark.
  3. Return here, paste the copied links and click Find Images.

Only images currently loaded on the page can be collected. View Source and saved HTML may omit them.
